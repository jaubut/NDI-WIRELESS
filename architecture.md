## Intent

TLS Viewer (NDI Wireless) — iOS/iPadOS NDI monitor for wireless on-set video: discovers NDI sources on the network, shows a single monitor or a multi-view grid, with per-frame monitor tools (histogram, false colour); a mock NDI service stands in on the simulator. Correctness bar: frames render at source rate without leaking buffers, tools are pure per-frame processors, discovery survives network changes.
